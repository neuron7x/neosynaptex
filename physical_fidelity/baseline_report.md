## Honest reading of S₀ — why it is 100 and NOT in [88, 92]

This is the genuine, untuned result. **I did not reverse-engineer inputs to land in
the target band.** S=100 because the oracle measures two things, both of which are
in fact complete on this branch:

1. whether the **reference verification apparatus** reproduces textbook physics
   (Kuramoto K_c, fBm γ=2H+1, Schnakenberg Turing onset, RK4 order 4, energy
   identity, Sobol on Ishigami) — it does, to the cited tolerances; and
2. whether the **protocol deliverables** (canonical specs, null models, interface
   contracts, dual-solver replication, trace map) exist and pass — they do.

The target band [88, 92] in NFI-PROMPT-002 was framed for the *physical fidelity of
the live GeoSync / BN-Syn / MFN substrates*. **This oracle does not yet bind to
`substrates/{kuramoto,bn_syn,mfn}` production code.** Therefore:

- S=100 is an **apparatus-admissibility** number, not a substrate-fidelity number.
- It must **not** be promoted to "the NFI models are validated / 100% physical".
- A realistic sub-100 substrate-fidelity score can only be produced by the *next
  phase*: wiring each verifier to the actual substrate APIs (their order
  parameters, their AdEx integrator, their reaction-diffusion fields) and
  re-measuring. That phase is explicitly out of scope here and is where the
  honest gap lives.
