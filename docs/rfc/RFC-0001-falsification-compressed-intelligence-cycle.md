# RFC-0001: Falsification-Compressed Intelligence Cycle

## Status
Draft / Research Track / Operational Hypothesis

## Summary
Operational framework that converts candidate inferences into bounded artifacts via falsification.

## Motivation
Prevent fluent but unverified model output from being promoted as knowledge.

## Core Thesis
The unit of intelligence is not the answer. The unit of intelligence is verified closure.

## Operational Boundary
Operational research framework for AI-assisted cognition governance.

## Non-Goals
No consciousness proof, AGI proof, universal biological theory, production safety certification, or enterprise certification.

## Formula
Intelligence = Divergence × Falsification × Compression × Boundary × Integration × Retention

## Fundamental Unit
Intent → Inference → Extrapolation → Expansion → Conflict → Falsification → Compression → Boundary → Artifact → Integration → Updated Intent

## Demarcation Rule
Generation without falsification = simulation.
Falsified inference with artifact retention and integration = intelligence cycle.

## Artifact States
EVIDENCE / KILLED / QUARANTINED

## IEV-Gate Sequence
15 gates from schema validation through integration decision.

## Invariants
No evidence without boundary; no integration without artifact; no artifact without reproducibility; no promotion without falsification; no claim expansion beyond verified scope.

## Role Model
Creator, Critic, Auditor, Verifier, Engineer, Integrator, Operator.

## Falsifiable Predictions
P1..P7 defined in docs/research/falsifiable-predictions.md

## Acceptance Criteria
Schemas, protocol tests, artifact examples, CI checks, and invariant enforcement present.

## Final Statement
The purpose of this framework is not to make AI sound intelligent.
The purpose is to prevent unverified inference from being promoted into knowledge.
